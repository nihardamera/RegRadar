Matching: a gap or partial rating from the key's circular that cites the key's policy in the same section (heading number); strict also requires the finding or action to state the rule's key fact (`KEY_FACT` in `regradar/evaluate_impact.py`, taken from the key's rule text). **Recall is not the whole story:** the pipeline flags many obligations, and precision is not measured. The key lists what was planted, not every shortfall, so unkeyed flags are neither right nor wrong until a person reviews them; they are listed in `impact_<model>.json`.
