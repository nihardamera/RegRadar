# RegRadar impact report: Frequency of reporting of credit information by Credit Institutions to Credit Information Companies

- Circular: `rbi-n12718`
- Model: `claude-haiku-5-5`; company: FlexiPay India (synthetic)
- Verdict: **action needed**
- Code checks: all passed
- 6 obligations: 1 met, 2 gap, 0 partial, 1 not covered, 2 not applicable; 0 for review
- Status: draft for human review

## Action plan

1. **Change FC-15 §2.1 so that credit information is updated and submitted to all four CICs fortnightly, as on the 15th and last day of each month.**
   - Priority P1; owner: Head of Credit, FlexiCredit; due: not stated in the circular
   - Rule (¶2): "CICs and CIs shall keep the credit information collected/maintained by them updated regularly on a fortnightly basis (i.e., as on 15th and last day of the respective month) or at such shorter intervals as mutually agreed upon between the CI and the CIC."
   - Policy: FC-15 §2.1
2. **Amend FC-15 §2.1 to require fortnightly submission to CICs within seven calendar days of each reporting fortnight.**
   - Priority P1; owner: Head of Credit, FlexiCredit; due: Within seven (7) calendar days of the relevant reporting fortnight
   - Rule (¶2): "The fortnightly submission of credit information by CIs to CICs shall be ensured within seven (7) calendar days of the relevant reporting fortnight."
   - Policy: FC-15 §2.1
