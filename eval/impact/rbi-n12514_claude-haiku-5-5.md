# RegRadar impact report: Guidelines on Default Loss Guarantee (DLG) in Digital Lending

- Circular: `rbi-n12514`
- Model: `claude-haiku-5-5`; company: FlexiPay India (synthetic)
- Verdict: **action needed**
- Code checks: all passed
- 21 obligations: 3 met, 1 gap, 4 partial, 12 not covered, 1 not applicable; 0 for review
- Status: draft for human review

## Action plan

1. **Reduce the DLG cover in FC-13 §6.1 to no more than 5% of the loan portfolio amount.**
   - Priority P1; owner: CEO, FlexiCredit; due: not stated in the circular
   - Rule (¶6): "RE shall ensure that total amount of DLG cover on any outstanding portfolio which is specified upfront shall not exceed five per cent of the amount of that loan portfolio."
   - Policy: FC-13 §6.1
2. **Add a requirement that every DLG arrangement is backed by a written, legally enforceable contract between FlexiCredit and FlexiPay before disbursement.**
   - Priority P2; owner: CEO, FlexiCredit; due: not stated in the circular
   - Rule (¶4): "DLG arrangements must be backed by an explicit legally enforceable contract between the RE and the DLG provider."
   - Policy: FC-13 §6.1
3. **Add a Board-approved DLG policy, adopted before any DLG arrangement, that sets out provider eligibility criteria, monitoring and review process, and fees payable to FlexiPay.**
   - Priority P2; owner: CEO, FlexiCredit; due: Before entering into any DLG arrangement
   - Rule (¶12.1): "REs shall put in place a Board approved policy before entering into any DLG arrangement. Such policy shall include, at the minimum, the eligibility criteria for DLG provider, nature and extent of DLG cover"
   - Policy: FC-13 §6.1
4. **Document in the Digital Lending Policy that the FlexiPay LSP relationship is a formal outsourcing arrangement under which the DLG is provided.**
   - Priority P3; owner: CEO, FlexiCredit; due: not stated in the circular
   - Rule (¶3): "RE may enter into DLG arrangements only with a Lending Service Provider (LSP)3/ other RE with which it has entered into an outsourcing (LSP) arrangement."
   - Policy: FC-13 §1.1, FC-13 §6.1
5. **Amend FC-13 §6.1 to state that the fixed deposit is maintained with a Scheduled Commercial Bank with a lien in FlexiCredit's favour.**
   - Priority P3; owner: CEO, FlexiCredit; due: not stated in the circular
   - Rule (¶5): "RE shall accept DLG only in one or more of the following forms: a. Cash deposited with the RE b. Fixed Deposits maintained with a Scheduled Commercial Bank with a lien marked in favour of the RE"
   - Policy: FC-13 §6.1

## Not applicable (1)

- In case of implicit guarantee arrangements, the DLG Provider shall not bear performance risk of more than the equivalent amount of five per cent of the underlying loan portfolio.: The 5% limit applies only to implicit guarantee arrangements, and the FlexiPay DLG is an explicit fixed-deposit guarantee.
