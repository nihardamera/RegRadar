## Action plan

1. **Remove the ₹10,000 exemption in FC-13 §3.2 so that every prospective borrower receives the standard-format KFS from Annex A before executing the loan contract.**
   - Priority P1; owner: CEO, FlexiCredit; due: not stated in the circular
   - Rule (¶4): "REs shall provide a KFS to all prospective borrowers to help them take an informed view before executing the loan contract, as per the standardised format given in the Annex A."
   - Policy: FC-13 §3.1, FC-13 §3.2
2. **Require the borrower's explicit consent before any fee or charge not mentioned in the KFS is levied at any stage of the loan term.**
   - Priority P1; owner: CEO, FlexiCredit; due: not stated in the circular
   - Rule (¶8): "Any fees, charges, etc. which are not mentioned in the KFS, cannot be charged by the REs to the borrower at any stage during the term of the loan, without explicit consent of the borrower."
   - Policy: FC-14 §4.1, FC-14 §3.2
3. **Exhibit the standard-format KFS as a summary box forming part of every loan agreement, including loans of ₹10,000 or less.**
   - Priority P1; owner: CEO, FlexiCredit; due: not stated in the circular
   - Rule (¶9): "The KFS shall also be included as a summary box to be exhibited as part of the loan agreement."
   - Policy: FC-13 §3.1, FC-13 §3.2
4. **Amend FP-09 so that a response to any complaint sent to the generic nodal email is given within 1 working day, separately from the 30-day resolution timeline.**
   - Priority P1; owner: Principal Nodal Officer; due: 1 working day
   - Rule (¶7): "RE may furnish generic email id, provided a response is made within 1 working day"
   - Policy: FP-09 §2.1, FP-09 §1.2, FP-09 §1.1
5. **Remove the ₹10,000 exemption in FC-13 §3.2 so that the harmonised standard-format KFS with APR is shown before signing for every retail and MSME term loan.**
   - Priority P2; owner: CEO, FlexiCredit; due: not stated in the circular
   - Rule (¶2): "The harmonised instructions shall be applicable in cases of all retail and MSME term loan products extended by all regulated entities (REs)."
   - Policy: FC-13 §3.1, FC-13 §3.2
6. **Amend FC-13 §3.1 so the KFS includes an APR computation sheet and the full amortisation schedule over the loan tenor, and state whether the §3.2 one-screen summary for small loans must also carry them.**
   - Priority P2; owner: CEO, FlexiCredit; due: not stated in the circular
   - Rule (¶6): "The KFS shall also include a computation sheet of annual percentage rate (APR), and the amortisation schedule of the loan over the loan tenor."
   - Policy: FC-13 §3.1, FC-13 §3.2
7. **Add a detailed disbursement schedule to the KFS for all loans and remove the replacement of the KFS for loans of ₹10,000 or less.**
   - Priority P2; owner: CEO, FlexiCredit; due: not stated in the circular
