## Action plan

1. **Amend FC-16 so the borrower receives notice of the proposed fraud classification and a fair opportunity to respond before the CRO classifies the account.**
   - Priority P1; owner: Chief Risk Officer; due: not stated in the circular
   - Rule (¶4.1.2): "Applicable NBFCs shall ensure that principles of natural justice are strictly adhered to before classifying / declaring an account as fraud"
   - Policy: FC-16 §2.2
2. **Amend FC-16 so that prior notice and an opportunity to be heard precede any fraud classification for sole lending accounts.**
   - Priority P1; owner: Chief Risk Officer; due: not stated in the circular
   - Rule (¶4.1.3): "Applicable NBFCs (sole lending, multiple banking arrangement or consortium lending) shall ensure that the principles of natural justice20 are strictly adhered"
   - Policy: FC-16 §2.2
3. **Change FC-16 §3.1 to require the fraud monitoring report to RBI within 14 days of classifying an incident or account as fraud, regardless of amount.**
   - Priority P1; owner: Chief Risk Officer; due: Immediately but not later than 14 days from the date of classification of an incident / account as fraud
   - Rule (¶6.2.1): "Applicable NBFCs shall furnish FMR27 in individual fraud cases, irrespective of the amount involved, immediately but not later than 14 days from the date of classification28 of an incident / account as fraud."
   - Policy: FC-16 §3.1
4. **Add a step to FC-16 requiring every early warning alert or trigger to be examined for whether the account needs a fraud investigation, with the outcome recorded.**
   - Priority P2; owner: Chief Risk Officer; due: not stated in the circular
   - Rule (¶3.3.2): "Generation of EWS alert(s) / trigger(s) shall necessitate examination whether the account needs to be investigated from potential fraud angle."
   - Policy: FC-16 §1.1
5. **Add a step to FC-16 requiring FlexiCredit staff to select the most appropriate fraud category from the listed categories when filing FMRs on the RBI portal.**
   - Priority P2; owner: Chief Risk Officer; due: not stated in the circular
   - Rule (¶6.1): "Applicable NBFCs shall choose the most appropriate category from any one of the following:"
   - Policy: FC-16 §3.1
6. **Add a step to FC-16 §2.1 requiring notice to the borrower and an opportunity to respond before the CRO classifies the account as fraud.**
   - Priority P2; owner: Chief Risk Officer; due: not stated in the circular
   - Rule (¶6.2.3): "The group entities will have to comply with the principles of natural justice before declaration of fraud31."
   - Policy: FC-16 §2.1
7. **Add a rule that the RBI/NHB fraud report must be filed before any fraud account is sold to another lender or ARC.**
   - Priority P2; owner: Chief Risk Officer; due: not stated in the circular
   - Rule (¶7.2): "In cases where Applicable NBFCs conclude that a fraud has been perpetrated in the account, they shall report it to RBI / NHB34 before selling the accounts to other lenders / ARCs35."
