# Customer Grievance Redressal Policy

## 1. Channels
1.1 Customers can complain in the app, by email to support@flexipay.example,
or on the toll-free helpline from 9 am to 9 pm, every day.

1.2 Every complaint gets a reference number by SMS within 1 hour.

## 2. Timelines
2.1 Complaints are resolved within 30 days. An unresolved complaint is
escalated to the Principal Nodal Officer after 15 days.

2.2 If a complaint is rejected in full or in part, or not resolved within
30 days, the reply tells the customer they may complain to the RBI under
the Reserve Bank – Integrated Ombudsman Scheme, and gives the link to the
RBI's complaint portal.

## 3. Failed transactions
3.1 A failed UPI or wallet transaction where the customer was debited is
reversed by T+1 day. If it is not, FlexiPay credits the customer ₹100 for
every day of delay, without waiting for a complaint.

3.2 A failed FlexiPay Checkout payment where the customer was debited is
refunded to the source within T+5 days.

## 4. Nodal officers
4.1 The name and contact details of the Principal Nodal Officer and the
grievance officer for FlexiCredit are shown in the app and on the website.

## 5. Review
5.1 The Customer Service Committee reviews complaint volumes, root causes and
turnaround times every quarter.
