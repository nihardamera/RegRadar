# Fair Practices Code and Loan Charges (FlexiCredit)

## 1. Applications and sanction
1.1 Loan application screens are in English and Hindi. Applicants are told
within 2 working days whether the loan is sanctioned. A rejected applicant is
told the main reasons in writing.

## 2. Interest
2.1 Interest rates are fixed for the life of the loan and set by the
Board-approved interest rate model, which takes account of the cost of funds,
the credit risk and the operating cost.

2.2 Interest is charged from the date of disbursal, not from the date of
sanction or agreement.

## 3. Charges for late payment
3.1 If an EMI is not paid on its due date, **penal interest** of 2% a month
is added to the loan's interest rate on the overdue amount until it is paid.

3.2 A bounce charge of ₹500 applies to each failed auto-debit.

## 4. Changes to terms
4.1 Any change to charges is told to borrowers at least 30 days in
advance, by SMS and in the app.

## 5. Recovery
5.1 Recovery follows FC-17. Borrowers are not harassed at any time.

## 6. Closure
6.1 When a loan is repaid in full, a no-dues certificate is issued in the app
within 7 days.

## 7. Review
7.1 The Board reviews compliance with this code every year.
