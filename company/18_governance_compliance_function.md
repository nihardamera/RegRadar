# Governance, Board Committees and Compliance Function

## 1. Boards
1.1 FlexiPay's Board has six directors, two of them independent.
FlexiCredit's Board has four directors, one of them independent.

## 2. Committees
2.1 **Audit Committee** (both companies): meets every quarter.

2.2 **Risk Management Committee** (both companies): meets every quarter;
covers credit, fraud, liquidity, operational and cyber risk.

2.3 **IT Strategy Committee** (group): chaired by an independent director;
meets once a year (FP-05 1.2).

2.4 **Customer Service Committee** (group): meets every quarter (FP-09 5.1).

2.5 **Nomination and Remuneration Committee** (FlexiPay): meets twice a
year.

## 3. Compliance function
3.1 The Chief Compliance Officer (CCO) is appointed by the Board for a
minimum term of 3 years, reports to the CEO and has direct access to the
Audit Committee.

3.2 The CCO keeps a register of every RBI direction that applies to the
group, with the internal policy that implements it and its owner.

3.3 Every new RBI circular is read by the compliance team within 5 working
days of issue. The CCO decides whether it applies, and assigns an owner and a
deadline for any change. This review is recorded in the compliance register.

## 4. Internal audit
4.1 Internal audit is outsourced to a chartered accountant firm and reports
to the Audit Committee. The annual plan covers payments, wallets, lending,
KYC and IT.

## 5. Policy reviews
5.1 Every policy has an owner and a review date. A policy is reviewed at
least once a year, and earlier when a regulation it implements changes.
