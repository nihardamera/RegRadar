# Cyber Incident Response Procedure

## 1. Severity
1.1 **Severity 1**: customer data exposed, payments unavailable for more than
30 minutes, or money lost. **Severity 2**: a contained intrusion or a partial
outage. **Severity 3**: anything else.

## 2. Response
2.1 The SOC raises an incident and pages the on-call security engineer.
For Severity 1 the CISO opens a bridge with the CTO, CRO and CCO within
30 minutes.

2.2 Contain, preserve evidence, eradicate, recover, then review.

## 3. Reporting
3.1 CERT-In is informed within 6 hours of the incident being noticed.

3.2 The RBI is informed of a Severity 1 or Severity 2 incident within
24 hours of detection, by email to the supervising department, followed by
the incident reporting format.

3.3 The sponsor bank and NPCI are informed of any incident that touches
UPI within 2 hours.

3.4 Customers whose personal data is affected are told within 72 hours.

## 4. Post-incident review
4.1 A root-cause analysis is written within 15 days of closure and goes to
the IT Strategy Committee at its next meeting.

## 5. Drills
5.1 A tabletop exercise is held once a year.
