# Customer Liability for Unauthorised Transactions (FlexiWallet)

## 1. Scope
1.1 Unauthorised electronic payment transactions from a FlexiWallet.

## 2. Liability
2.1 **Zero liability** when the fraud is due to FlexiPay's negligence or a
breach in the system, whether or not the customer reported it.

2.2 **Zero liability** when the fault is with neither FlexiPay nor the
customer, and the customer reports it within 3 days of being told about the
transaction.

2.3 **Limited liability** when the customer reports within 4 to 7 days: the
customer bears the transaction value or ₹10,000, whichever is lower.

2.4 When the customer reports after 7 days, or the loss is due to the
customer's negligence (sharing an OTP or PIN, for example), the customer
bears the whole loss until they report it.

## 3. Reversal
3.1 A reported amount is credited back to the wallet as a shadow reversal
within 10 days of the report.

3.2 The complaint is resolved, and liability decided, within 90 days of the
report. If it is not, the customer is compensated as if they had zero
liability.

## 4. Alerts
4.1 Every debit triggers an SMS and in-app alert. Customers can report an
unauthorised transaction by replying to the alert, in the app or on the
helpline, 24 hours a day.
