# KYC and AML Policy

## 1. Scope
1.1 Applies to FlexiWallet Full-KYC holders, FlexiCredit borrowers and
FlexiPay Checkout merchants (merchant due diligence is in FP-03). FlexiWallet
Small holders are covered by the minimum-details rules in FP-11.

1.2 Only resident Indian individuals aged 18 or over are onboarded as retail
customers. NRIs, PIOs and foreign nationals are declined at the eligibility
step.

## 2. Customer identification
2.1 Default route: Aadhaar OTP-based e-KYC with PAN verification against the
Income Tax database. Accounts opened by OTP-based e-KYC in non-face-to-face
mode are limited to a balance of ₹1,00,000 and must complete full
identification within one year, or they are closed.

2.2 Video-based Customer Identification Process (V-CIP) is used for borrowers
with loans above ₹50,000 and for customers who ask for full KYC without
Aadhaar OTP. A trained FlexiPay official conducts the session live, sees
the original PAN card on camera, asks randomised questions and captures a
geo-tagged live photograph.

2.3 V-CIP sessions are recorded and stored by our video-KYC vendor,
KYCStream Pte Ltd, on its servers in Singapore, for 5 years.

2.4 Officially valid documents accepted: passport, driving licence, voter
ID card, proof of possession of Aadhaar, NREGA job card and the letter of the
National Population Register.

## 3. Risk categorisation and periodic updation
3.1 Customers are rated low, medium or high risk at onboarding using
occupation, income, product and geography. Politically exposed persons are
always high risk and need CCO approval.

3.2 KYC is updated at least once every 2 years for high-risk customers,
every 8 years for medium-risk and every 10 years for low-risk customers.

## 4. Monitoring and reporting
4.1 Transactions are monitored against scenarios approved by the CCO.
Suspicious Transaction Reports (STRs) are filed with FIU-IND within 7 days of
arriving at the conclusion that a transaction is suspicious.

4.2 Cash Transaction Reports do not apply, as FlexiPay handles no cash.

4.3 Customer names are screened against the UN Security Council sanctions
lists and the lists under section 51A of the UAPA at onboarding and whenever
the lists change.

## 5. Records
5.1 Identification records and transaction records are kept for at least
5 years after the business relationship ends or the transaction is completed,
whichever is later.

## 6. Ownership
6.1 The CCO is the Principal Officer and the Designated Director is the CEO.
The policy is approved by the Board and reviewed every year.
