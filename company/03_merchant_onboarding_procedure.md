# Merchant Onboarding and Due Diligence Procedure

## 1. Who we onboard
1.1 Online merchants that sell goods or services in India: sole
proprietors, partnerships, LLPs and companies.

1.2 We do not onboard merchants in the prohibited categories in Annex A
(gambling, unlicensed lending, adult content, crypto-asset trading,
counterfeit goods, and any business barred by law).

## 2. Due diligence
2.1 Every merchant completes KYC as a legal entity (PAN, certificate of
incorporation or registration, GST certificate where applicable, and KYC of
the beneficial owners and authorised signatories).

2.2 Small merchants with annual card and UPI turnover below ₹5 lakh can be
onboarded with a PAN, a bank account in their name verified by penny drop,
and a self-declaration of their business, without a site check.

2.3 Merchant websites are reviewed before go-live for terms and conditions,
a refund and cancellation policy, a privacy policy and customer support
contact details.

## 3. Risk rating and monitoring
3.1 Merchants are rated low, medium or high risk by category, turnover and
chargeback history. High-risk merchants are reviewed every 6 months.

3.2 Transaction monitoring flags spikes in volume, refunds above 10% and
chargebacks above 1% of transactions for review within 2 working days.

## 4. Storage of card data
4.1 FlexiPay Checkout does not store card numbers. Card-on-file transactions
use network tokens. Merchants are contractually barred from storing card
data.

## 5. Agreements
5.1 Each merchant signs the FlexiPay Merchant Agreement, which covers
refunds, chargebacks, the escrow and settlement terms in FP-12, and
FlexiPay's right to suspend settlement during investigation of fraud.

## 6. Review
6.1 This procedure is reviewed every two years by the Head of Merchant Risk.
