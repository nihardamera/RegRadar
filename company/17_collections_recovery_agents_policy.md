# Collections and Recovery Agents Policy (FlexiCredit)

## 1. Who collects
1.1 Overdue loans are collected first by FlexiCredit's in-house team by app
notification, SMS and phone, then by recovery agencies empanelled by the
Head of Collections.

## 2. Agencies and agents
2.1 Agencies are checked before empanelment, and their agents' police
verification is done by the agency.

2.2 Agents complete the IIBF debt recovery agent certificate.

2.3 The borrower is told the name and contact details of the agency when the
account is passed to it, in the app and by SMS.

## 3. Conduct
3.1 Calls and visits are allowed between 8 am and 9 pm.

3.2 Agents may not use threats, abusive language, or contact the
borrower's family, friends or colleagues.

3.3 All collection calls are recorded.

## 4. Complaints
4.1 A complaint about a recovery agent is handled under FP-09. Agents
under investigation are taken off FlexiCredit accounts.

## 5. Review
5.1 The Head of Collections reviews agency performance and complaints every
month.
