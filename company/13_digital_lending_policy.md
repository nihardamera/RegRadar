# Digital Lending Policy (FlexiCredit)

## 1. Roles
1.1 FlexiCredit is the lender. FlexiPay is its only Lending Service Provider
(LSP), and the FlexiPay app is the Digital Lending App (DLA).

1.2 The FlexiPay app and website name FlexiCredit as the lender on every
loan screen and link to FlexiCredit's website.

## 2. Disbursal and repayment
2.1 Loans are disbursed directly into the borrower's own bank account.
Repayments are made by the borrower directly to FlexiCredit's account.
No money passes through a FlexiPay pool account.

2.2 FlexiPay is paid its fees by FlexiCredit, not by the borrower.

## 3. Key Fact Statement
3.1 A Key Fact Statement (KFS), in the standard format, is shown before the
loan agreement is signed. It shows the annual percentage rate (APR), the
recovery mechanism, the grievance officer and the cooling-off terms.

3.2 For loans of ₹10,000 or less, the KFS is replaced by a one-screen
summary of the amount, the EMI and the due dates, to keep the journey short.

## 4. Cooling-off and prepayment
4.1 A borrower may cancel a FlexiCredit Instant loan within 3 days of
disbursal by repaying the principal and the interest for the days used.
A prepayment fee of 3% of the principal applies during the first 30 days.

4.2 PayLater lines have no cooling-off period.

## 5. Credit limits
5.1 PayLater limits are reviewed every quarter. Limits can be raised
automatically for customers with a good repayment record; the customer is
told by SMS.

## 6. Default loss guarantee
6.1 FlexiPay provides FlexiCredit a default loss guarantee (DLG) of 8% of
the amount disbursed in each quarterly portfolio, as a fixed deposit with a
lien in FlexiCredit's favour.

## 7. Data
7.1 Data is collected with explicit consent and stored in India, as set out
in FP-08.

## 8. Grievance
8.1 FlexiCredit's grievance officer handles lending complaints, as set out
in FP-09. A complaint not resolved within 30 days can go to the RBI under the
Integrated Ombudsman Scheme.
