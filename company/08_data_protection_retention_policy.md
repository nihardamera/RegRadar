# Data Protection, Storage and Retention Policy

## 1. Storage in India
1.1 All payment system data (customer details, payment credentials and
transaction records) is stored only on servers in India: AWS Mumbai
(ap-south-1), with disaster-recovery copies in AWS Hyderabad (ap-south-2).

## 2. Consent and purpose
2.1 Consent is taken at onboarding, in plain language and per purpose. Data
is used only for the purpose it was collected for.

2.2 The FlexiPay app reads SMS only to verify the mobile number when it binds
the device. For lending it asks for one-time access to the camera and
location during KYC, and does not access files, media, contact lists or call
logs.

## 3. Sharing
3.1 Customer data is shared with third parties only with explicit consent
or where the law requires it.

3.2 FlexiCredit shares borrower data with credit information companies as
required (FC-15).

## 4. Retention
4.1 Transaction records are kept for 10 years. KYC records are kept as set
out in FP-02. Application logs are kept for 1 year.

4.2 Data no longer needed is deleted within 90 days of its retention period
ending.

## 5. Customer rights
5.1 Customers can see, correct and ask for deletion of their data in the app
or by email to privacy@flexipay.example. Requests are answered within
30 days.

## 6. Data Protection Officer
6.1 The Data Protection Officer reports to the CCO and is the contact for
the Data Protection Board of India.
