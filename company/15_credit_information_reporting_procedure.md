# Credit Information Reporting Procedure (FlexiCredit)

## 1. Members
1.1 FlexiCredit is a member of all four credit information companies (CICs)
and reports to each of them.

## 2. Frequency
2.1 Credit information for all borrowers is submitted to the CICs once a
month, within 7 days of the month end.

## 3. Data quality
3.1 Rejected records are corrected and resubmitted with the next monthly
file.

## 4. Disputes and correction
4.1 A borrower's request to correct credit information is resolved within
30 days. If it is not, the borrower is compensated ₹100 a day for the delay.

## 5. Notice to borrowers
5.1 A borrower is told by SMS and email before being reported as in default
to the CICs.

## 6. Ownership
6.1 The Head of Credit is the nodal officer for CICs.
