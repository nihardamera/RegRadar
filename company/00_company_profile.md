# FlexiPay India: Company Profile

## 1. The group
1.1 FlexiPay India Private Limited ("FlexiPay") is a payments company
incorporated in 2019 and headquartered in Bengaluru.

1.2 FlexiCredit Finance Private Limited ("FlexiCredit") is FlexiPay's wholly
owned subsidiary. It is registered with the Reserve Bank of India as an
NBFC – Investment and Credit Company (NBFC-ICC) in the Base Layer, and it does
not accept public deposits.

## 2. Authorisations and roles
2.1 FlexiPay is authorised by the RBI under the Payment and Settlement Systems
Act, 2007 as an online Payment Aggregator (PA-O). Final authorisation was
granted in March 2023.

2.2 FlexiPay issues semi-closed system prepaid payment instruments (PPIs):
FlexiWallet Small and FlexiWallet Full-KYC.

2.3 FlexiPay is a UPI Third Party Application Provider (TPAP). Its sponsor
bank is Coastal Commercial Bank Limited (fictional).

2.4 FlexiPay is the Lending Service Provider (LSP) for FlexiCredit. The FlexiPay
app is the Digital Lending App (DLA) through which FlexiCredit's loans are
offered.

## 3. Scale (31 March 2026)
3.1 3.2 million monthly active users; 41,000 onboarded merchants.

3.2 Monthly UPI volume of 38 million transactions. FlexiPay Checkout processes
₹2,150 crore a month for merchants.

3.3 FlexiCredit's loan book is ₹510 crore across 2.3 lakh borrowers. Its total
assets were ₹560 crore in the audited balance sheet of 31 March 2026.

3.4 FlexiPay's net worth was ₹16 crore on 31 March 2026, the end of the third
full financial year since it was authorised as a PA.

## 4. People and functions
4.1 Chief Executive Officer; Chief Compliance Officer (CCO); Chief Risk
Officer (CRO); Chief Information Security Officer (CISO); Chief Technology
Officer; Head of Customer Experience; Principal Nodal Officer (grievances).

4.2 FlexiCredit has its own CEO and a Head of Credit, and shares the CCO, CRO
and CISO with FlexiPay under a group services agreement.
