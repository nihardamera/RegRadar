# Fraud Classification and Reporting Procedure (FlexiCredit)

## 1. Early warning signals
1.1 Loan accounts are watched for early warning signals: first-EMI default,
identity documents linked to other defaulted loans, and many applications
from one device.

## 2. Classification
2.1 When the Fraud Monitoring Team finds evidence that a borrower obtained
a loan by fraud, the CRO classifies the account as fraud within 7 days.

2.2 The borrower is told of the classification after it is made.

## 3. Reporting
3.1 Frauds are reported to the RBI within 21 days of detection.

3.2 Frauds above ₹1 crore are reported to the police.

## 4. Oversight
4.1 The Risk Management Committee of the Board reviews frauds every
quarter.

## 5. Staff accountability
5.1 Staff accountability is examined for every fraud above ₹10 lakh.
