# Fraud Risk Management Policy (FlexiPay)

## 1. Scope
1.1 Fraud on FlexiUPI, FlexiWallet and FlexiPay Checkout. Fraud in
FlexiCredit's loan accounts is handled under FC-16.

## 2. Governance
2.1 The Risk Management Committee of the Board oversees fraud risk and
reviews fraud data every quarter.

2.2 The fraud risk function reports to the CRO and is independent of the
business teams.

## 3. Prevention and detection
3.1 A rules engine screens every transaction in real time for velocity,
amount, device and location anomalies. Rules are fixed thresholds set by the
fraud team and reviewed every quarter.

3.2 Alerts are reviewed by fraud operations within 4 hours, 24 hours a day.

3.3 Mule-account indicators (many inbound credits from unrelated payers,
immediate outward transfers, new devices) put a wallet on hold until a
customer call confirms the activity.

3.4 FlexiPay shares confirmed fraudulent UPI IDs and phone numbers with the
sponsor bank and NPCI the next working day.

## 4. Reporting
4.1 Payment frauds are reported to the RBI through the Central Payments Fraud
Information Registry (CPFIR) within 7 days of detection.

4.2 Customers are advised to report cyber fraud on the National Cyber Crime
Reporting Portal or helpline 1930.

## 5. Customer awareness
5.1 In-app messages and SMS campaigns warn customers every month about
common frauds (fake KYC calls, screen-sharing apps, QR codes for receiving
money).

## 6. Review
6.1 The policy is approved by the Board and reviewed every year.
