# Payment Aggregator Settlement and Escrow Procedure (FlexiPay Checkout)

## 1. Escrow account
1.1 All amounts collected from customers for merchants are credited to
FlexiPay's escrow account with Coastal Commercial Bank. FlexiPay's own funds
are never mixed with it.

1.2 Permitted credits: payments from customers, refunds and reversals from
merchants, and pre-funding by FlexiPay. Permitted debits: payments to
merchants, refunds to customers and FlexiPay's commission at the agreed rate.

## 2. Settlement
2.1 Merchants are settled on a T+3 basis, where T is the day the customer
pays, whether or not FlexiPay or the merchant is responsible for delivery.

2.2 Settlement can be held for a merchant under fraud investigation (FP-03
5.1).

## 3. Reconciliation
3.1 The escrow balance is reconciled with merchant payables every day by
10 am the next working day. Differences above ₹10,000 go to the Head of
Finance Operations the same day.

## 4. Interest
4.1 Under the agreement with the bank, FlexiPay may keep up to 15% of the
escrow balance as a fixed deposit with Coastal Commercial Bank, and receives
the interest on it.

## 5. Audit
5.1 A chartered accountant certifies every quarter that the escrow account
was operated as these rules require, and the certificate is sent to the
bank.

## 6. Net worth
6.1 Finance tracks FlexiPay's net worth every quarter and reports it to the
Board. Net worth at the end of FY 2025-26 was ₹16 crore (FP-00 3.4).
