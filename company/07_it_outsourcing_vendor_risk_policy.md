# IT Outsourcing and Vendor Risk Policy

## 1. Scope
1.1 Every arrangement in which a vendor runs IT services for FlexiPay or
FlexiCredit: cloud hosting (AWS Mumbai), the outsourced SOC, the V-CIP vendor,
SMS and email gateways, and the card tokenisation service.

## 2. What is never outsourced
2.1 Decisions on KYC acceptance, credit sanction and compliance, and
ownership of customer data.

## 3. Due diligence before contract
3.1 Financial standing, security certifications (ISO 27001 or SOC 2), data
location, sub-contractors, and the vendor's business continuity plan.

## 4. Contracts
4.1 Every contract gives FlexiPay and the RBI the right to audit and inspect
the vendor, requires data to be kept confidential and returned on exit, and
sets service levels.

4.2 Material vendors cannot sub-contract without FlexiPay's written consent.

## 5. Monitoring
5.1 Material vendors are reviewed every two years against their service
levels and an information security questionnaire.

## 6. Concentration and exit
6.1 Each material vendor has a documented exit plan. Cloud exit is tested
by restoring a backup to a second provider once every three years.

## 7. Register
7.1 The CTO keeps a register of all IT outsourcing arrangements and their
risk rating.
