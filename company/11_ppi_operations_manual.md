# PPI Operations Manual (FlexiWallet)

## 1. Opening
1.1 FlexiWallet Small opens after OTP verification of the mobile number
and a self-declared name and identity document number.

1.2 A FlexiWallet Small must be converted to FlexiWallet Full-KYC within
24 months of issue. After 24 months no further loads are allowed; the
customer can still use or transfer out the balance.

## 2. Limits
2.1 Limits are as in FP-01: FlexiWallet Small ₹10,000 outstanding,
FlexiWallet Full-KYC ₹1,00,000 outstanding.

## 3. Loading
3.1 Wallets are loaded from bank accounts, debit and credit cards, UPI and,
for customers with a PayLater line, from FlexiCredit PayLater (FP-01 4.2).

3.2 Card loads use the card network's additional factor of authentication.

## 4. Interoperability
4.1 FlexiWallet Full-KYC is interoperable through UPI: holders can pay any
UPI QR code and link the wallet to third-party UPI apps.

## 5. Escrow
5.1 Wallet balances are held in an escrow account with Coastal Commercial
Bank. The escrow balance is reconciled with outstanding wallet balances at
the end of every day.

## 6. Expiry and closure
6.1 Wallets do not expire. A wallet with no financial transaction for
12 months is marked inactive; it is reactivated after re-verification.

6.2 On closure, the balance is transferred to the customer's bank account
after verification, free of charge.

7.1 See FP-04 and FP-10.
